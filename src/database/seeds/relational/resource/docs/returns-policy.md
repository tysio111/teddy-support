# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery, for any reason. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with their original tags attached.

The following items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they are faulty: underwear, swimwear with a removed hygiene liner, earrings, and face masks. Gift cards cannot be returned.

## How to return an item

1. Start a return in your account under "Orders", or ask our support assistant to start a return for you with your order number.
2. Choose a return method: a prepaid InPost or DPD return label (Poland only), or send the parcel yourself.
3. Pack the items securely, ideally in the original packaging, and drop the parcel off within 7 days of starting the return.

## Return shipping costs

Returns within Poland using our prepaid label are free. Returns from other countries are sent at your own cost; we recommend a tracked service, because we cannot refund parcels that never reach us.

Faulty or incorrectly sent items can always be returned for free, from any country. Contact us first and we will send you a prepaid label.

## Refunds

We inspect returned items within 3 business days of receiving them. Once the return is approved, we refund the item price to the original payment method. Card and BLIK refunds usually appear within 3-5 business days; bank transfers can take up to 10 business days.

The original shipping cost is refunded only if you return the whole order, or if the item was faulty or sent by mistake. Express delivery surcharges are never refunded.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To get a different size or colour, return the item for a refund and place a new order. If the new size is out of stock, you can subscribe to a back-in-stock notification on the product page.

## Damaged or faulty items

If an item arrives damaged or develops a fault within 2 years of purchase, you are covered by the statutory warranty. Send us your order number and photos of the problem, and we will offer a repair, replacement, or refund.
