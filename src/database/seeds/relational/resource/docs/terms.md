# Terms and conditions

## Who we are

The online shop is operated by Example Fashion sp. z o.o., based in Poznań, Poland. These terms apply to all orders placed in our online shop.

## Placing an order

An order is a binding offer to buy. The contract is concluded when we send the order confirmation email. We may cancel an order if an item turns out to be out of stock or was listed at an obviously wrong price; in that case we refund any payment in full.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until it leaves the warehouse, in your account or by asking our support assistant. Once the order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but you can return it under the returns policy.

## Right of withdrawal

Consumers in the EU have the right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason. Our voluntary 30-day return policy extends this period.

## Prices

All prices include VAT. Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay. For deliveries outside the EU, import duties and local taxes may apply and are paid by the recipient.

## Complaints

Complaints about faulty products are handled under the statutory warranty, which lasts 2 years from delivery. We respond to complaints within 14 days.

## Personal data

We process personal data according to our privacy policy, only for handling orders, customer support and, if you agreed, marketing. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time.

## Account

You are responsible for keeping your account password confidential. You can change the email address of your account in the account settings or by asking our support assistant.
