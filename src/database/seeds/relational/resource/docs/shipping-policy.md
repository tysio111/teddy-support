# Shipping policy

We ship from our warehouse in Poznań, Poland. Orders placed on a business day before 14:00 (CET) leave the warehouse the same day; later orders ship the next business day.

## Where we ship

We ship to all European Union countries, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and Norway. We do not currently ship outside Europe. Orders to the UK, Switzerland and Norway may be subject to import duties and VAT, which are paid by the recipient on delivery.

## Delivery options and costs

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (DPD) | 2-4 business days in Poland, 3-6 business days elsewhere in the EU | 14.99 PLN in Poland, 9.99 EUR elsewhere |
| Express (DHL) | Next business day in Poland, 1-3 business days elsewhere in the EU | 29.99 PLN in Poland, 24.99 EUR elsewhere |
| Parcel locker (InPost) | 1-2 business days, Poland only | 11.99 PLN |

Standard shipping is free for orders over 299 PLN in Poland and over 79 EUR elsewhere in the EU. Free shipping does not apply to express delivery.

Delivery times to the UK, Switzerland and Norway are 5-8 business days, and only standard delivery is available there.

## Tracking your order

As soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse you receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking usually starts showing updates within 24 hours. You can also ask our support assistant for the status of an order using your order number (for example ORD-100234).

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address as long as the order has not left the warehouse yet. Once the parcel is on its way, the address can only be changed with the carrier, using the link in the tracking email.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If your parcel has not arrived within 10 business days of shipping, contact us. We open an investigation with the carrier, and if the parcel is confirmed lost we send a replacement or refund the order in full, including shipping costs.
