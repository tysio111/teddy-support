# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods

We accept the following payment methods:

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- BLIK (Poland only)
- Fast bank transfer via Przelewy24 (Poland only)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Buy now, pay later with Klarna (Poland and Germany, orders between 100 PLN and 5000 PLN)

We do not accept cash on delivery.

## Currencies

Prices are shown in PLN in Poland and in EUR in the other EU countries. Orders to the UK, Switzerland and Norway are charged in EUR. If your card uses a different currency, your bank converts the amount and may charge a conversion fee.

## When you are charged

Card, BLIK, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are charged when you place the order. If an order is cancelled before it ships, the charge is refunded in full to the same payment method within 3-5 business days.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order stays reserved for 60 minutes so you can retry with the same or a different payment method from your account. After that, the order is cancelled automatically and the items return to stock.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is attached to the shipping confirmation email. To get an invoice for a company, enter your company name and VAT number at checkout. Invoices cannot be changed to a company invoice after the order has shipped.

## Payment security

We never store full card numbers. Card payments are processed by our payment provider and protected by 3-D Secure, so your bank may ask you to confirm the payment in its app.
