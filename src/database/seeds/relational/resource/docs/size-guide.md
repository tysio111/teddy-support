## Jeans

Jeans are sized by waist and inside leg length in inches, for example 30x32 means a 30-inch waist and a 32-inch inside leg. To find your size, measure your natural waist and the inside of your leg from the crotch to the ankle.

## Shoes

Shoes use EU sizes from 36 to 46. Our sneakers run half a size small, so we recommend ordering half a size up. Boots and sandals are true to size.

## How to measure

Use a soft measuring tape and measure over light clothing. Measure the bust or chest at the fullest point, the waist at the narrowest point, and the hips at the widest point. Keep the tape snug but not tight.
